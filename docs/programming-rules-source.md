## Step 2. Member Programs (Programming Coach)

### A. Build Program (TeamBuildr)

During the week, before the next training week:

- ☐ Open the member in TeamBuildr
- ☐ Copy previous phase program over to next week
- ☐ Update exercises and rep ranges based on progression

**Default Locker Room progression (typical):**  
10–12 → 8–10 → 6–8 → 4–6  

May change based on goals, injuries, or strength phases.

### B. Apply Duration Of Program (Supabase)

After program is complete:

- ☐ Go to Supabase
- ☐ Completion Weeks Column
- ☐ Select program duration  
  **Standard:** 6 weeks  
  **Adjustable:** 4 to 6 weeks depending on the member  

Admin extends the program at the end of the week based on your selection.

## How to Write A Program

These rules exist to help coaches deliver the highest quality session, with smooth logistics on the floor.

### Rule 1: Maximum Two Exercises per Series

**Purpose:** Keep sessions flowing, avoid clutter and difficult set up.

**Guideline:** Only two exercises can be paired inside any series.

- **Correct Example:** A Series: Press and Row  
- **Incorrect Example:** A Series: Press and Row and Sit Up

### Rule 2: Pairings Must Work at Both Gym Locations

**Purpose:** Ensure any program can run at both Bridge and Bligh Street without equipment conflict.

**Examples of Pairings That Do NOT Work:**

- Barbell Squat and Leg Curl — Works at Bligh but not Bridge
- Flat Press and Leg Curl Machine — Could end up at opposite ends of the gym at Bligh
- Barbell Squat paired with Barbell Press or DB Press — Too much equipment and setup in one spot  

Really important we think logistics when designing program pairings.

### Rule 3: C-Series Must Be Self-Sufficient + Minimum Equipment

**Purpose:** If the session runs over time, members must be able to continue independently without using key equipment and risk injury. (We aim to minimize machine and bench usage in C-Series to prevent floor overflow.)

**Good C-Series Options:** Arm work, Core exercises  

**Avoid in C-Series:** Hip thrusts, Leg press, Any exercise requiring a bench, Any machine likely needed for the next class

### Rule 4: Home Workouts Must Be Loaded on Weekends

**Purpose:** Avoid confusion for coaches delivering at home/out of Lockeroom sessions.

**Exceptions:** If the client prefers the home workout on the exact day they will do it, then clearly label it so coaches know not to coach it (Example: *Home Workout / Day to Complete on Your Own*).

### Rule 5: No New Exercises Added Without Approval

**Purpose:** Prevent overload of the exercise library and keep delivery consistent on the floor.

**Guideline:** Any new exercise must be:

- Posted in this chat and
- Approved by Head Coach or Programming Lead  

before being added to programs.

### Rule 6: Exercises to leave out when possible

- Walking lunges  
- Farmer carries

## Programming Rules

### Session Timing

- Every session is 40 minutes including warm-up
- Programs should not exceed 35 minutes of work time  
- **Typical time splits:**  
  Option 1: 15 minutes A-series, 10 minutes B-series, 10 minutes C-series  
  Option 2: 20 minutes A-series, 15 minutes B-series  

### Set Structures

- **Standard:** 3-set A-series, 3-set B-series, 2-set C-series
- **For quicker clients:** 4-set A-series, 2-set B-series, 2-set C-series
- **Other options:** 4 to 5-set A-series and 3 set B-series only; B capped at 3 sets max

### Series Composition

- No pairing of single-limb upper with double-limb upper (same rule for lower)
- Single-limb upper can be paired with double-limb lower and vice versa
- Only one single-limb series per day

### Warm-Up Sets

- Sparing in B and C-series
- Only add for advanced clients with heavy lifts

### Rest Times

- Under 6 reps: 120 seconds (possible 180 but needs to fit within session)
- 7 to 12 reps: 90 seconds
- 12+ reps: 75 seconds
- Coaches’ discretion, but rest should be 60 to 120 seconds where appropriate

### Daily Programming Rules

- If program is fully bilateral, some clients can handle 3-3-2 or 4-2-2 sets
- If there is single-limb work, cap at 3-2-2 sets for A, B, C

### Additional Work To Do On Own

- Extra client work must be clearly labelled in the title
- Should be simple, minimal setup, and easy for clients to do unsupervised

### Rehab Integration

- Rehab work counts toward total set volume
- Must be factored into overall workload
